❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is InternTrack? +

InternTrack is a student-focused internship management platform that helps you organize applications, interviews, deadlines, tasks and career progress.

Can I track multiple internships? +

Yes. You can add and manage multiple internship applications and track each application independently.

Can I track interview schedules? +

Yes. You can track interview dates, rounds, notes and preparation activities.

Will I receive deadline reminders? +

Yes. The application can notify you about upcoming deadlines, interviews and other important activities.
